Fishing Report

Seasonal updates, conditions, and club reminders.

March 1, 2026 • Conditions Update

Early signs of ice-off on the lower lakes

We’re seeing signs of an abnormally early ice-off this year. The lower lakes have started to melt along the edges, and there are a few small seams of open water developing in the most sun-exposed areas.

As of 3/1/2026, there is still less than ~30% open water across the lower lakes. The remaining surface is still locked up with ice. Nighttime temperatures are hovering around freezing, so we anticipate it will be a couple more weeks before conditions open up for consistent fishing and we begin guided trips.

When the melt accelerates, we’ll keep updates simple: what’s open, what’s fishable, and what kind of day to expect. As soon as we see reliable access and safe, consistent water, we’ll begin scheduling guided days.

Open Water< 30% (lower lakes)
Overnight LowsNear freezing
Guided TripsLikely in ~2+ weeks

Member reminder

Members are welcome to two complimentary guided trips annually (up to 4 people per trip). If you’d like to get on the early-season list, email your preferred dates and group size and we’ll follow up as conditions improve.

We’ll update again as soon as ice-off progresses enough for consistent fishing.
