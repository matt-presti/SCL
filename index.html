About the property

Located in Saguache County, Colorado, Silver Creek Lakes Club is a private fishing property at the headwaters of the Arkansas River Valley. Fishing takes place on a series of lakes, ponds, and a shallow stream connection between them—ideal for stillwater tactics, indicator fishing, and streamer days.

Water

3 lakes • 10 ponds • interconnecting stream

Access

Membership via land/home ownership; guests allowed with members

Options

Independent days or guided excursions

Guided days available

Want a dialed approach for stillwater, stream connections, or a river float? Book a guided day with our local team.

  • Half-day or full-day options (customizable)
  • Stillwater strategy: depths, retrieves, and lake structure
  • Streamers, indicators, and seasonal approaches
