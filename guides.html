Guide Bios

Local experience, thoughtful instruction, and a low-stress day on the water—whether you’re here for stillwater, stream connections, or a river trip.

Matthew Presti

Hometown: Pueblo, Colorado

Lifelong fly fisherman from Pueblo, CO. Grew up fishing the Arkansas and Gunnison rivers, with experience across the West. Matthew loves Southern Colorado’s sacred waters and friendly community.

Former guide in Boulder and Rocky Mountain National Park, now guiding home water for Silver Creek Lakes. A sucker for big browns, and always happy to get a day dialed—whether it’s stillwater tactics, streamers, or a classic float.
