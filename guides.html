Guiding

Experienced, local, and focused on a great day

Whether you want to learn stillwater fundamentals, refine your streamer game, or simply enjoy a relaxed day on private water, we take a professional, safety-first approach—without overcomplicating it.

Matthew Presti

Southern Colorado native • Hometown: Pueblo, Colorado

Matthew is a Southern Colorado native who grew up fishing the Arkansas River and exploring the waters of the region from Pueblo to the upper valley. He’s an avid fly fisherman and boater with a style rooted in the classics: reading water, controlling depth, and staying adaptable as conditions change.

After guiding in Northern Colorado and building experience across a range of rivers and stillwaters, Matthew now guides in the Arkansas Valley—especially the home waters of Silver Creek Lakes Club. He enjoys helping anglers become more confident on stillwater: working structure, using deliberate retrieves, and understanding when to switch tactics.

He’s a sucker for big browns, loves a well-planned boat day, and offers guided days on the property plus select Arkansas River trips when conditions align.

Jacob Meier

Hometown: Grand Lake, Colorado

Jacob is an experienced guide and lifelong fly fisherman from the Grand Lake / Granby area, where his seasons revolve around stillwater trout, streamer fishing, and time on the water in all weather. He’s known for a calm teaching style and for simplifying what matters: where fish hold, how they feed, and how to present effectively.

Jacob is especially skilled at stillwater systems—finding productive depth zones, fishing structure, and dialing retrieve speed. When rivers are in shape, he’s an avid streamer fisherman and loves helping anglers learn how to move fish with purpose, from clean casting mechanics to controlling swing and strip cadence.

On guided days he emphasizes practical takeaways: building a plan, troubleshooting efficiently, and leaving you with a repeatable approach you can use on your own waters.
