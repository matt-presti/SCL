About the club

Silver Creek Lakes Club is a private fly fishing property in Saguache County, Colorado at the headwaters of the Arkansas River Valley. Fishing occurs across a connected network of lakes, ponds, and shallow stream sections.

  • Membership: available only through owning land or a home
  • Guests: allowed with members (policies and fees vary)
  • Access: independent fishing and guided days available

Guiding inquiries

If you’re requesting a guided day, share your goals (learn stillwater fundamentals, target larger fish, general instruction, etc.). We can recommend timing, approach, and a simple gear list.
