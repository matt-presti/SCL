About Silver Creek Lakes Club

Silver Creek Lakes Club is a private fly fishing property in Saguache County, Colorado at the headwaters of the Arkansas River Valley. Fishing occurs across a connected network of lakes, ponds, and the shallow stream between them.

Membership

Membership is available only through owning land or a home within the club.

Guests

Guests are allowed with members. Policies and fees may vary—email for details.

Newsletter

Available upon request. Email us to join.

Guiding

Guided days are available on the property, and select river trips may be available depending on conditions. We’ll coordinate a plan based on season, weather, and your goals.
